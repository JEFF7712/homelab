## P1: Brace screws still cannot fasten to the columns

`column.py:100-201` cuts rod bores, sockets, rail rebates, and M3 rail slots. It contains no receiving M4 brace holes, inserts, or nut pockets. Moving bars outside the columns solves bar collision only.

Independent 4 mm diameter screw-shank probes, over 10 mm of potential column engagement:

- Left side attachment at Y=22.5, Z=145, from X=0 toward the column: **125.6637 mm3** collision, the entire probe.
- Rear upper attachment at X=23, Z=335, Y=190..200: **71.4269 mm3** collision.

Specify fastener type, length, receiving geometry, bearing face, and tool access. The existing collision tests do not model these screws.

## P1: New corner mounts omit an attachment bearing surface

`column.py:251-276` constructs a 30 mm deep body. Extending narrow edge lips and their bridge does not extend the plate beneath X=22.8 at the second attachment point.

For the front mount, Y=38 lies beyond the body at Y=0..30. For the rear mount, Y=157 lies before the body at Y=170..200. Subtracting screw holes at those coordinates does not create mounting material.

A 7 mm diameter, 0.2 mm deep bearing probe immediately below the specified head-seat height gives:

| Mount / Y | Material under head envelope |
| --- | ---: |
| Front / 25 | 4.516 mm3 |
| Front / 38 | **0 mm3** |
| Rear / 157 | **0 mm3** |
| Rear / 170 | 2.258 mm3, partial edge support |

These probes establish absent or partial bearing geometry, not a strength rating. Restore supported attachment pads at every measured T1 screw location.

## P1: Accepted locating channel was changed

The new outer lip inner face is X=0. The inner lip starts at X=`COLUMN_INWARD_MM + LIP_CLEARANCE_MM = 29.15`. Thus the gap is **29.15 mm**, versus the accepted **30.3 mm** channel for the 30 mm stock member. The new geometry uses column width in place of the stock-member width.

The lips also extend toward the transverse member without the previous `CORNER_LIP_START_MM` relief. Restore the tested interface geometry before claiming compatibility. Prior physical acceptance cannot be transferred to these changed mounts.

## P1: Right rails remain on the left in the assembly

`rail.py:95-105` mirrors the right rail around the strip's own center, leaving its X bounds at **13..29**, the same as the left rail. The upper wrapper does likewise. `model.py:1140-1143` inserts them into the master compound without placement corrections.

The lower left/right rail solids overlap by **18197.9849 mm3**. Their expected assembled right-side region is X=252..268. The right-column/right-rail test passes because the misplaced rail is far from that column. It never asserts actual attachment or checks the two rails against each other.

Use one authoritative assembly builder for export and verification. Check intended contact, hole coaxiality and minimum bearing material in addition to empty intersections.

## Verification claims that need narrowing

- `model.py:1066-1149` includes printed components but no M3/M4 screws, inserts, washers, nuts, retainers, or tie rods. Tie rods are constructed in tests, not included in the STEP. The claim that the exported assembly contains positioned fasteners is incorrect.
- The 36 pairs are selected collision probes, not a complete fastening or all-pairs assembly audit. For example, the lower crossbar is checked against lower columns that begin above it; its actual mount attachment is not proved.
- Top closure and lid/handle fastening remain undefined. No current test demonstrates complete fore-aft restraint.
- Bed-envelope tests do not establish face-down orientation, brim fit, bridge quality or support removal. Rear crossbars are now 251 mm long, leaving only 5 mm total nominal axis margin before brim or machine exclusions.
- The rebate was changed from an 8.6 mm back face to 9.7 mm. Prior coupon clamping acceptance predates that change and cannot establish the revised bearing-wall thickness or rail seating.

Correct the remaining geometry and assembly placement before new production prints. Then verify actual fastener envelopes and bearing/contact paths, followed by slice inspection and focused physical checks only where the design changed. No full-frame load or printability approval is established by this audit.

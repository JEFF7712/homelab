# Revision 3 production audit

Verdict: not ready for the production print roster.

Audit of the current uncommitted CAD on 2026-09-25. No CAD or STL files changed. User-reported physical acceptance of the rail and column coupons is accepted as evidence for those coupons, not for geometry they omit.

## P1: Production blockers

### 1. Top washer cannot enter the upper column

`column.py:184-203` cuts a 20.5 mm washer cavity beneath a 12 mm diameter access neck. The specified washer is 20 mm OD. It cannot pass through that neck. This reintroduces the trapped washer problem corrected in the Phase 2 top hardware design.

Build123d intersection of the actual 20 x 1.2 mm washer envelope with the upper column at the top entrance: **241.274 mm3**. The bearing cavity also starts 1.4 mm below the nominal seat, changing the clamp stack. Restore an accessible loading path and recheck seat location, nut/tool access, and rod length.

### 2. Full rails collide with the base mount and middle spigot

The rail is 9.5 mm thick, but `_rail_rebate` at `column.py:55-67` cuts from Y=-1 to Y=8.6. The physically seated rail therefore belongs at Y=-0.9, rather than the exported Y=0. Using that seated placement removes ordinary rail/column overlap, but does not remove these collisions:

| Pair | Solid intersection |
| --- | ---: |
| Lower rail / Phase 2 mount | 2831.280 mm3 |
| Upper rail / lower-column spigot | 940.513 mm3 |

The lower rail starts at Z=12, within the old mount, whose body has no rail rebate. The lower column's rebate ends at Z=170.85 while its spigot extends to Z=191.85, obstructing the upper rail. See `rail.py:lower_equipment_rail`, `column.py:132-145`, and `model.py:582-650`.

The accepted Z=55..99.45 coupon does not contain either region. Carry the accepted rail fit into revised end and splice geometry; do not repeat the same coupon.

### 3. Brace connections are absent and parts occupy the same space

`bracing.py:26-99` supplies clearance holes in the bars. Neither full column builder defines matching brace holes, inserts, nut traps, or mounting reliefs. The old base mount also has no designed rear crossbar connection.

At the source's assembly coordinates, the left side bar intersects the front lower column by **1026.496 mm3** and upper column by **617.054 mm3**. The upper rear bar intersects a rear upper column translated to Y=170 by **874.142 mm3**. Changing rear-post facing cannot supply the missing fastener interfaces.

Side fastener axes are exactly at the Z=169.85 splice. A connection must be designed around that joint, rather than simply adding a hole on the part boundary. Rear crossbar hole X=27.5 is only 1.5 mm from the nominal X=29 column edge, less than its 2.25 mm clearance radius. These locations need actual load-bearing attachment geometry.

### 4. Rear diagonal does not match available anchors

`bracing.py:102-145` hardcodes two 205 mm pieces, a 28 mm lap and 10 mm end margins. The assembled end-hole distance is:

`205 + 205 - 28 - 10 - 10 = 362 mm`.

Opposite existing crossbar holes are separated by X=226 and Z=305 mm:

`sqrt(226^2 + 305^2) = 379.606 mm`.

It is **17.606 mm short** for those anchors. No separate diagonal anchor pattern or installed diagonal transform is defined. Derive brace length from selected assembled anchor coordinates and include the endpoint hardware, not only the two lap bolts.

### 5. Four-corner mounting and top closure are unfinished

The roster prescribes four copies of one front corner mount. `model.py:602` uses only `ATTACH_Y_MM[:2]`, Y=25 and 38. Mirroring these through the 200 mm rack depth gives 175 and 162, rather than the measured rear locations 170 and 157. The four mounts need explicit variants/placements; one accepted front mount is insufficient evidence.

No Revision 3 connected top perimeter or lid/handle attachment is included. Mid-height side links alone do not establish resistance to fore-aft sway. Define the complete frame and load paths before releasing posts.

### 6. The verification does not test the claimed assembly

`test_cad.py:14-22` constructs Phase 2 uprights for the existing mating, rod and washer tests. Revision 3 additions at lines 129-174 check isolated solid validity and bed fit. They do not test Revision 3 assembly collisions, washer insertion, or brace fastening.

`model.py:1060-1071` exports a compound of rails and bars, omitting columns, mounts, diagonals and hardware. Right rail wrappers return parts in local left-side coordinates, and this compound does not place them on the right posts. It is not a complete assembled STEP proof. Valid isolated solids do not imply a buildable frame.

## P2: Remaining printability and documentation findings

- The full columns are solid-section CAD around the rod bore and pockets, not hollow tubular columns as the docstring claims. CAD material volumes are approximately 96.65 cm3 lower and 119.83 cm3 upper. These are not sliced filament estimates; infill settings determine actual consumption.
- Upright sockets still end in abrupt roofs, including the approximately 21.5 x 22.5 mm upper socket around the rod bore. The top washer undercut and horizontal nut slots also need layer inspection. Support-free full-column printing has not been demonstrated by the new mid-column coupon.
- The two diagonal halves need individual face-down orientations because their lap cuts are on opposite faces. Generic 'flat on bed' instructions do not specify this sufficiently.
- M3 counterbore and adjacent M5 pilot centers are 6.35 mm apart on the same X axis. Their radii sum to 6.35 mm, leaving zero nominal separating wall at the counterbore depth. A 6.7 mm insert envelope extends farther. Coupon acceptance is useful, but this is not the positive clearance implied by `test_model.py:test_rail_fasteners_clear_rack_inserts`, which checks only a 5 mm center-distance threshold.
- The source explicitly specifies **8U added height, 7 complete standard mounting U**, plus partial positions. One accepted 1U pattern does not establish eight usable U or verify full left/right spacing.
- The quoted bottom hardware assembly sequence omits the bottom washer. Its placement must precede installation onto the T1, as described by the existing Phase 2 instructions.
- Final fastener lengths, rod cutting, retained preload, payload and lateral-load acceptance remain unresolved. Initial insert torque does not establish long-term frame capacity.

## Evidence and limits

Inspected current CAD, parameters, export function, README and regression test source. Ran targeted Build123d 0.13.0 solid-intersection probes, including rails seated at the actual rebate depth, and recomputed brace endpoints. Measured all 16 binary STL bounding boxes: individual axis extents are below 256 mm. This confirms part envelopes, not plate layouts with brims or support settings. Did not run a slicer, the full regression suite, a mesh/source equivalence check, or a physical load test.

Accepted results retained: 6.2 mm insert fit, local 1U equipment alignment, M3 nut loading and clamping, and local M5 rod passage. Middle male/female solids also have zero intersection in the CAD probe. Newly identified collisions occur outside the new coupon region.

## Required next revision

Complete a positioned four-corner assembly, resolve mount/rail/spigot interference, restore top washer access, and design actual brace connections with endpoint-derived lengths. Extend geometric checks to that Revision 3 assembly. Slice each final print orientation. Only newly changed interfaces need new focused coupons. Production approval must remain separate from the reported successful coupon fits.

## P1: Transverse-member lip relief remains missing

`column.py:313-328` runs the front inner lip from Y=0 through Y=42, at X=30.3..32.3 and Z=-3..0 below the seating plane. This occupies the front transverse member's envelope at Y=0..30.

Independent Build123d probe using the recorded 281 mm width, 30 mm member width, and the uppermost 3 mm beneath the seating plane:

- Old `phase2_corner_mount`: **0 mm3** intersection.
- New `rev3_corner_mount_front_left`: **180 mm3** intersection.

This is a collision against the rectangular stock-frame envelope used by the design, not a fresh measurement of the physical rack. The previous accepted mount specifically stopped its lips beyond `CORNER_LIP_START_MM`; the new mount does not use that relief. Restoring channel width alone does not preserve its accepted corner seating. Provide corresponding front/rear relief and verify all four variants against the stock frame.

## P2: Rear screw heads are not seated in the hardware model

`bracing.py:60-70` cuts the rear crossbar counterbores down to Y=206. `model.py:1094-1108` starts the rear attachment screw shanks at Y=210, placing their head undersides there. The head-bearing plane is therefore **4 mm above the counterbore floor**. A zero-intersection check can pass while the screw is unseated.

Move hardware to its actual bearing plane and recompute engagement and tip clearance. With the current M4x16 model seated at Y=206, the tip reaches Y=190, exactly the end of the 10 mm column pilot starting at Y=200. The side M4x16 model likewise consumes 6 mm of bar plus the full 10 mm receiving bore. Neither modeled stack leaves tip clearance.

The crossbar attachment paths are also labeled and cut as insert pilots, while matching receiving pilots exist in the columns. Specify clearly where the inserts go and which member is a clearance hole so the hardware can clamp the joint. Actual insert OD, length, installation depth, and screw dimensions need to drive this check. `M4_HEAT_SET_DEPTH_MM=9.5` is currently not used to construct an insert envelope or verify engagement.

## Remaining assembly and release limits

- Tie rods and the selected M4 screws are now included, as claimed. The hardware model still omits inserts, M3 rail fasteners, tie-rod nuts/washers/retainers, and diagonal lap fasteners. Do not interpret it as complete hardware validation.
- A connected top frame/lid interface and complete fore-aft stiffness proof remain absent from the Revision 3 release assembly. These were already open gates, not newly introduced requirements.
- Revised mount seating, changed rail rebate, and new M4 insert fits have not been physically accepted in this exchange.
- Column socket roofs, nut slots, and final part orientations still need actual sliced-layer inspection. Bed-envelope success does not prove support-free printing.
- Full-frame payload, retained preload and lateral-load performance remain unverified. Do not infer them from successful coupons or collision tests.

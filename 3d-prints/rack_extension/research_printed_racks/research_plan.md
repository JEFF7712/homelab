# Reuse research plan

Question: Which existing printed rack designs offer verifiable, reusable geometry
or construction ideas for the measured DeskPi T1 upper extension, while reducing
support material and avoiding premature full-column prints?

1. Printed 10-inch modular racks: source CAD availability, upright sections,
   joints, print orientation/support requirements, hardware, reported builds,
   and explicit licensing. Findings: `findings_printed.md`.
2. Hybrid and modular frame construction: printed corners with metal members,
   threaded-rod clamping, removable bracing and frame connections. Seek directly
   reusable mechanisms and explain footprint/height tradeoffs. Findings:
   `findings_hybrid.md`.

Use creator repositories, model pages and build documentation as primary
evidence. Each researcher has a budget of 3-5 web searches, with direct source
inspection for strong candidates. Distinguish documented printing/build use
from tested load ratings and unverified performance claims.

Synthesis: compare candidates against the current 281 x 200 mm envelope,
355.6 mm added height, approximately 236 mm hole spacing, 223 mm column opening,
M5 rods and inserts, A1 print volume, and physically accepted mating geometry.
Recommend concrete changes to the present design, identify what can be reused
unchanged, and state what requires adaptation or physical verification.
Do not alter the accepted CAD while conducting this comparison.
